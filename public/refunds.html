Free access and previous payment support

VeltaCapital is free as of September 5, 2026.

There are no paid plans, subscriptions, checkout pages, or automatic renewals. All signed-in learners receive the same access.

No payment information required

VeltaCapital does not request or store payment card information. Learning credits are earned through educational activity and cannot be purchased or exchanged for money.

Support for an earlier payment

If you made a payment before September 5, 2026, or believe you were charged in error, email veltacapitalhq@gmail.com with the account email and receipt identifier. Do not send a card number or other sensitive payment information by email. Requests will be reviewed under the terms in effect when the payment was made and applicable law.

Equal access for existing accounts

Previous purchase status does not provide different features. Existing and new accounts now receive the complete course, flashcards, unlimited historical chart exercises, simulation portfolio, and learning rewards without charge.

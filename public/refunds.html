Purchase and refund details

Plans are one-time purchases, not subscriptions.

The listed price is charged once in USD through Stripe. VeltaCapital does not create an automatic renewal for Course Trial, Starter, Standard, or Premium access.

Check your plan before purchasing

The pricing page states the chapter limit, historical chart exercise allowance, and included learning features for each plan. Hypothetical outcomes, portfolio values, certificates, and learning credits have no cash value.

Refund and purchase support

For a refund request, duplicate charge, access problem, or unexpected charge, email veltacapitalhq@gmail.com with the email used at checkout and the Stripe receipt identifier. Do not send a card number or other sensitive payment information by email. Eligibility for a refund is subject to applicable law and any additional terms displayed at checkout.

Checkout availability

Price options may be displayed while checkout is not yet enabled for a particular deployment. If checkout is unavailable, no payment is taken. A plan is granted only after Stripe confirms a completed payment.
